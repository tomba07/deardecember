EVERYDAY LIFE, REMEMBERED

A glimpse of everyday life.

What did your grandparents do on an ordinary Sunday? What was always on the kitchen table? What made them laugh? Small details like these can be hard to find later.

Miniography is a place to keep those details from your own life: the routines, favourite things, people and places that feel familiar now. One day, your children or someone generations from now might enjoy discovering what an ordinary day was like for you.

Each door offers a few questions to get you started. Write down what you remember, however small. It can simply be how things were.

Your words stay in this browser. No account, no advertising, no central collection of your entries. Keep them for yourself; sharing is always your choice.

Where is my journal saved? Can I switch devices?

Your entries are saved automatically in this browser. To keep a backup or move to another device, download your journal, transfer the file yourself and open it in Miniography on the other device. There is no automatic sync.

Opening a journal file replaces the journal in that browser after you confirm. Download the current one first if you want to keep it. If your profile is unfinished, use “Backup unfinished draft” below.
