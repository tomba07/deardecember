Dear December✳
SMALL MOMENTS, KEPT

A little room for reflection.

A memory that makes you smile. A person who comes to mind. Something you see differently now. Take a quiet moment to reflect and put a few thoughts into words.

Small moments are a good place to begin: a familiar routine, a favourite place or a conversation you remember. There’s room for bigger experiences too. Over time, these reflections can offer your children or future generations a glimpse of life as you knew it.

Each door offers a few questions to get you started. Follow what comes to mind and write as much or as little as you like.

Your words stay in this browser. No account, no advertising, no central collection of your entries. Keep them for yourself; sharing is always your choice.

Where is my journal saved? Can I switch devices?

Your entries are saved automatically in this browser. To keep a backup or move to another device, download your journal, transfer the file yourself and open it in Dear December on the other device. There is no automatic sync.

Opening a journal file replaces the journal in that browser after you confirm. Download the current one first if you want to keep it. If your profile is unfinished, use “Backup unfinished draft” below.
